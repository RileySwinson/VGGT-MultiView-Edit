Abstract

Text-driven editing of 3D Gaussian splatting scenes commonly edits rendered views one at a time with a 2D diffusion editor and fits the scene to the results. Because each view is edited independently, the views disagree, and the fitted scene averages them into a weak and inconsistent edit. Many approaches to multi-view consistency sacrifice this iterative refinement, and those that propagate edits through the scene's depth face a further difficulty: once an edit changes the object's shape, the depth of the original scene no longer matches the edited views. We warp already-edited views into the view being edited using camera and depth information, and inject the warped content into the editor's latent during the early denoising steps. All views are first edited once in a chain ordered by overlap, after which the Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer (VGGT) estimates depth from these edited views, which is then used for propagation throughout GaussianEditor's iterative loop. Our method generally improves multi-view consistency (MEt3R), has no result with CLIP directional similarity, and has generally higher-qualiy edits compared to GaussianEditor.
